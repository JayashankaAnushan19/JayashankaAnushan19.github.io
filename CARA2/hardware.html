Power

A custom 18650 battery pack (Samsung INR18650-32E cells, about 12 V) feeds the Arduino, sensors, motor driver and GSM module through buck/boost converters; the Jetson has its own adapter. Runtime under the current configuration has not been measured yet.

Most of V1’s hardest faults traced back to power distribution — peripherals that worked alone and failed when everything ran together. Characterising this properly is EXP-02.

Cost

Prototype bill of materials: $409.10 against a $500 ceiling; about $446.60 including modules bought and later replaced during V1.

Mechanical

The chassis covers, camera tower, sensor mounts and wire-management parts were designed in SolidWorks; the source files are in Hardware_Creation/ in the code repository.
