Patient identification and following

EXP-05Registered 8 October 2026

Can a low-cost single-camera pipeline keep the right person framed and at a comfortable distance in a shared home?

Why this matters

Following only the enrolled person — not visitors — is what makes CARA a companion rather than a camera on wheels. V1 got it working; V2 needs to measure it.

Evidence so far

  • A correct LBPH match scored 78.66 against a 70 threshold; raising it to 85 lifted face lock to 101 of 110 frames (31 Aug 2026).
  • Face-lock rate: 79.1 % in mid light, 32.4 % in low light with a 31.7 s dropout (23 Sep 2026).
  • Follow-me uses face area (8–20 % of the frame) as a distance proxy; Haar detection fails when a face fills the frame (7 Oct 2026).
  • Multi-person discrimination was never formally tested in V1.

Planned method

  • Scripted following trials and multi-person sessions.
  • Compare LBPH with a modern lightweight face-embedding model on the same footage.

Measures

  • Lock percentage
  • Wrong-person locks
  • Distance error
  • Recoveries after loss
