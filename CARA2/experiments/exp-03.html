GSM alert delivery under a constrained supply

EXP-03Registered 8 October 2026

What supply margin and retry policy give reliable SMS alert delivery within 30 seconds of a confirmed event?

Why this matters

SMS is CARA’s safety channel — the reason it doesn’t need Wi-Fi. V1 showed it works, but never measured how reliably or how fast.

Evidence so far

  • Bench sweep (25 Sep 2026): 6/6 SMS delivered at 6.5 V; AT-command corruption at 6.4 V; corruption and brief network deregistration at 6.2–6.3 V.
  • On the assembled robot, hardware Serial1 (pins 18/19) was silent while Serial2 (16/17) worked (27 Sep 2026).
  • Timed delivery trials (test IDs G-01 to G-05) were never run in V1.

Planned method

  • At least 15 timed trials from event confirmation to SMS arrival, with voltage logged at the module pins during transmission.
  • Compare single-attempt sending with the firmware’s 3-attempt retry policy.

Measures

  • Delivery rate
  • Latency median / max
  • Share delivered under 30 s
  • Supply sag during transmit

Status history

  • 8 October 2026 — Registered as proposed.
