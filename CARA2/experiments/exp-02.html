Peripheral power architecture

EXP-02Registered 8 October 2026

How does supply topology — Arduino 5 V rail, dedicated regulator, or the Jetson’s USB port — affect RFID, servo and ultrasonic reliability?

Why this matters

Several V1 faults appeared only when the whole robot was running and disappeared on the bench. A single, measured account of how supply topology affects each peripheral would turn a string of individual fixes into a design rule.

Evidence so far

  • The MFRC522 read a corrupted VersionReg (0x24) and detected no tags inside the full firmware, but read 0x92 and detected tags in isolation (4 Oct 2026).
  • A seven-configuration isolation test in V1 traced RFID tag-detection failure to powering the Mega through the Jetson’s USB port — a power requirement rather than a component fault.
  • Five of six ultrasonic sensors read a constant 0.00 cm while sharing the Arduino 5 V rail (6 Sep 2026).

Planned method

  • Fixed test matrix of supply configurations, everything else held constant.
  • Per configuration: repeated tag presentations, ultrasonic dropout count, rail voltage under load.

Measures

  • Tag detection rate
  • VersionReg integrity
  • Ultrasonic zero-reading rate
  • Rail sag (V) under load
