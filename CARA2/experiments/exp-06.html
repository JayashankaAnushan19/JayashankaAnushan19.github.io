Drive train and in-place turning

EXP-06Registered 8 October 2026

What limits in-place turning on this chassis, and can it be fixed mechanically or with closed-loop control?

Why this matters

A companion robot that cannot turn reliably cannot follow anyone around a home. V1 documented the limit; V2 should explain and remove it.

Evidence so far

  • One side stalls during in-place turns under both open-loop and encoder PI control (V1 conclusion).
  • Straight driving moves at 150 PWM; turning needed 220 PWM (4 Oct 2026).
  • Encoder left/right mapping found crossed and corrected (6 Oct 2026).
  • About 60 % of serial status lines were corrupted once the motors ran (20 Sep 2026).

Planned method

  • Per-side current and encoder-logged spin trials.
  • Variants: higher-torque motors, wheel/caster friction changes, motor-noise suppression.

Measures

  • Achieved yaw rate
  • Stall frequency
  • Motor current
  • Serial corruption rate
