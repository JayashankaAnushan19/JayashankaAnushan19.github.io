Vision-based fall detection in realistic conditions

EXP-04Registered 8 October 2026

How accurate and how fast is pose-based fall detection on the robot itself under realistic lighting, framing and occupancy?

Why this matters

Fall detection is CARA’s headline capability, but its accuracy figures come from offline images, and the audit found the final demonstration did not use the trained classifier at all.

Evidence so far

  • Deployed RandomForest model (644 samples): 87.6 % test / 89.6 % 5-fold CV accuracy, 14.7 % false-positive rate — offline.
  • Confirmation time fell from ~20 s to ~0.3–0.6 s when consecutive checks were cut from 3 to 1 (ROS2 node, 25 Sep 2026), at the cost of more single-spike false alerts.
  • In low light the pose model was never evaluated for 31 s (no tracked target) and the camera dropped to ~7.5–8.7 fps (25 Sep 2026).
  • In the final V1 configuration the dashboard process logged “No trained ML classifier found” and used the rule-based fallback.

Planned method

  • Per condition: 20 staged falls + 20 everyday activities, at several lighting levels.
  • Load the deployed classifier in the live path and log fall-to-alert latency.
  • False-positive rate per hour with an occupant present.

Measures

  • Accuracy, recall, precision
  • False positives per hour
  • Fall-to-alert latency
