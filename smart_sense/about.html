Our Story

Be Smart With Us

The story behind the softest little stress relievers you'll find.

How it started

Handmade by Rashmi Dunukara, one squishy at a time.

Smart Sense began as a small, personal project — a way to bring a little softness and joy into everyday life. What started with a handful of hand-shaped Taba Squishy toys shared on TikTok and Facebook quickly grew into a page loved by hundreds of followers.

Every single squishy is still made, packed, and dispatched by hand. From choosing the colours to wrapping each order with care, Smart Sense stays true to its roots: small-batch, handmade, and made with genuine love for the people who order from us.
