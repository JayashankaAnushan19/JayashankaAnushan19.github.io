Overview

At RoboJain, we believe robotics should be accessible, practical, and built for real-world needs. We specialise in developing DIY to medium-scale robotic solutions across a wide range of price points — making innovation affordable and achievable for businesses, institutions, and individuals alike.

Whether it's hardware, software, or a fully integrated system, we transform ideas into engineered realities with precision, creativity, and cost-effectiveness. Innovation at RoboJain is powered by a focused team working across electronics, mechanical design, and software — building solutions that are flexible, professional, and aligned with the goals of the people they're built for.

Our mission is simple: turn imagination into reality. From a small DIY project to a custom automation tool or a medium-scale robotic system — we work through feasibility, timelines, methods, and cost together, so every solution is tailored, efficient, and built to last.

Log

Heads-down, building.

The past several months have been quiet on purpose. Hardware, firmware, testing, iteration — real engineering work, not just concepts.

We're building robotic systems that operate without Wi-Fi dependence, without enterprise budgets, and without assuming the end user is a tech expert. Most assistive and service robots on the market were designed for environments with reliable infrastructure and high price tolerance. We're designing for everyone else.
