Overview

LoanMaster is a full-featured software platform designed specifically for microfinance companies — covering every aspect of financial operations from the moment a loan application is submitted to the day the final repayment is confirmed.

Unlike generic accounting software, LoanMaster is purpose-built for lending institutions. It understands loan schedules, interest calculation methods, penalty structures, and the day-to-day workflows of borrower management — without requiring the organisation to adapt its processes to fit the software.

Whether you operate from a single branch or manage a distributed network of lending officers across multiple locations, LoanMaster scales with your operation and keeps every stakeholder — from field officers to management — working from the same accurate, up-to-date financial data.

Features

Everything a lending institution needs.

📋

Loan Origination & Management

Handle the complete loan lifecycle — from application intake and credit assessment through disbursement approval and final closure — in one unified workflow.

👤

Borrower Profile & KYC

Maintain rich borrower profiles with identification documents, contact details, guarantor information, and full loan history — all searchable and auditable.

📅

Repayment Schedule Tracking

Automated repayment schedules with daily, weekly, or monthly cycles. Real-time tracking of paid, due, and overdue instalments across the entire portfolio.

📐

Interest & Penalty Engine

Flexible interest calculation — flat rate, reducing balance, or custom — with configurable grace periods, late-payment penalties, and fee structures.

🏢

Multi-branch Operations

Manage multiple branches from a single system. Branch-level reporting, inter-branch transfers, and consolidated dashboards give management full visibility.

👥

Role-based Staff Access

Assign permissions by role — loan officers, branch managers, auditors, and administrators each see exactly what they need to do their job, nothing more.

📊

Financial Reports & Analytics

Portfolio performance reports, collection efficiency, PAR (Portfolio at Risk) analysis, and cash flow summaries — exportable and ready for audit or board review.

📒

Payment History & Ledger

Complete transaction ledger for every loan account — immutable, timestamped records of every disbursement, repayment, fee, and adjustment.

🔔

Alerts & Overdue Tracking

Automatic flagging of overdue accounts, upcoming maturity dates, and portfolio anomalies — so your team stays ahead of collection issues before they escalate.
