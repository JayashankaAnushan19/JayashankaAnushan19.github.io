ROS & autonomy

ROS 2-based control architectures, navigation stacks, and on-device AI deployment for real-time decision making.

Why RoboJain

Autonomy has to hold up outside the lab. RoboJain designs for environments without reliable infrastructure — where a robot has to keep making decisions on its own.

Monitoring matters as much as autonomy: engineers and operators need a single interface to watch live sensor data and send commands back.

How it will work

From conversation to working system.

Every project — DIY or medium-scale — moves through the same four stages.

01

Discuss

We talk through the problem, constraints, and what "done" actually looks like for you.

02

Scope

Feasibility, timeline, and cost are laid out clearly before any build work begins.

03

Build

Hardware, firmware, and software come together — tested iteratively, not all at once at the end.

04

Deploy

The system goes where it's needed, with documentation and support for the people using it.
