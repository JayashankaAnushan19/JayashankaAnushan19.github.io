Camera Tower Complete — Full Assembly Testing Next

The camera tower assembly started in Week 07 was finished, along with the remaining wire connections on the aluminium chassis. Full assembly testing — everything mounted and running together — is next, and this post will be filled in further as the week continues.

Camera Tower and Wiring Complete

The camera tower and the rest of the wire connections were finished, bringing the chassis to a state ready for a full assembly test — everything mounted together rather than tested piece by piece as in Week 07.

Camera Gear Fix — Newly Printed Part Checked and Tested

A replacement gear for the camera pan mechanism came back from the university's printer this week. It was fitted onto the servo, checked for mesh and play against its mating gear, and run through its full range of motion before being called done and put back into the tower head.

What Did Not Go Well (So Far)

No carryover issues from the GSM side this week — the SMS/registration test resolved in Week 07 stayed resolved. Effort instead went entirely into finishing the camera tower, its gear fix, and the remaining chassis wiring.
