18–24 Sep 2026 Week 15 Hardware In Progress

Final Wiring Done — Next Up, Everything Together on the New Battery

The week opens closing out the wiring pass that spilled over from Week 14's motor and encoder work: the final wiring on the new 4-motor/encoder setup is declared fixed and complete, and a dedicated mount was designed for the custom battery pack. The next step is the real milestone — connecting everything together on the new battery and starting actual integrated testing, not another isolated bench rig.

This week is still in progress. This post reflects the work done so far; more will be added as the rest of the week happens.

Final Wiring Declared Complete

The wiring on the 4-motor, 2-encoder setup mapped out and physically connected in Week 14 got its final fixes today, closing out that thread. This is a hardware-side call, not something re-verified independently here — the real test is the integration run coming up next, not a claim on its own.

A Battery Clip for the Custom Pack

A new mounting part — Battery Clip (All/Bottom/Top — a two-piece clip design) — was designed to physically hold the custom 18650 battery pack from Week 14 in place on the chassis, rather than leaving it loose. The wire-hiding cover's support bar from Week 13 was revised again alongside it, to fit around the final wiring run.

What Did Not Go Well (So Far)

Nothing broken this session, but nothing physically integration-tested yet either — the final wiring and the new battery clip are both declared done on the hardware side, not proven by actually running the robot on them together. Everything carried in from Week 14 is still open: the new 4WD encoder wiring has no firmware behind it, the RTC driver remains completely unverified, the LCD+RTC shared-bus assumption hasn't been bench-tested with both live at once, the dashboard's admin password is still the unchanged default, and the custom battery pack itself hasn't been run under real load.
