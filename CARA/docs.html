Safety Design

Safety in CARA is implemented across three layers, informed by ISO 13482:2014 (safety requirements for personal care robots), which defines risk classes and mitigation approaches for robots that physically co-exist with humans.

Physical Collision Safety

12 × HC-SR04 sensors provide continuous 360° coverage with 50ms polling. Emergency stop at 20cm threshold. This threshold was calculated based on maximum operating speed (0.3 m/s) and motor deceleration response time (~80ms) to guarantee stopping before contact.

Non-overridable: Obstacle avoidance takes precedence over all commands, including remote drive and ALERT state. No software path bypasses this check.

Functional Safety — Fall Detection

Two-stage confirmation prevents false-positive SMS flooding: (1) 2-second hold period — all three posture conditions must persist continuously before fall is confirmed; (2) 30-second cooldown after each triggered alert — prevents repeated SMS from transient posture re-entry.

Target false positive rate: <2 per hour of continuous monitoring in typical home environment.

Electrical Safety

SIM800L powered on a dedicated 4.0V rail, isolated from Arduino 5V bus. 1000µF capacitor absorbs TX burst transients. LiPo battery has integrated protection circuit preventing overdischarge (<3.0V/cell) and short-circuit protection.

Staircase / Edge Safety

Three TCRT5000 downward-facing IR sensors detect loss of floor surface. Emergency stop triggered if 2+ sensors simultaneously show no floor reflection. Prevents the robot from driving off a step edge or into a staircase during autonomous navigation.

Research Prototype Notice

CARA v1.0 is a research prototype evaluated in a university lab environment. Real-world eldercare deployment would require full ISO 13482 certification, extended trials with elderly participants, and clinical safety review. The safety design described here is appropriate for the defined research scope.
