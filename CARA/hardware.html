Pins 2 & 3 (SoftwareSerial) $15–35

Critical: GSM Module Power Design

The GSM module (SIM800L or SIM76xx) draws up to ~2A during SMS transmission — far beyond what the Arduino 5V pin can supply. It requires a dedicated 4.0V regulated supply from a separate LM2596 buck converter (not the Arduino rail), with a 1000µF electrolytic capacitor across its power terminals to absorb current spikes. Without this, the module resets during transmission.
