Communication

ComponentModelSpecificationRolePinsEst. Cost
GSM Module A7670C (4G/LTE) Multi-band LTE · AT commands · ~3.4–4.2V supply · ~2A peak TX current SMS emergency alerts and check-ins with zero Wi-Fi requirement. Runs on the Arduino's hardware Serial1, not SoftwareSerial. Swapped from SIM800L (2G) after discovering Du/Etisalat have decommissioned 2G in the UAE — SIM800L remains a valid, cheaper choice for a Sri Lanka deployment where 2G coverage is still active. Serial1 (TX=18/RX=19) · PWRKEY=9 $15–35

Critical: GSM Module Power Design

The GSM module (SIM800L or A7670C) draws up to ~2A during SMS transmission — far beyond what the Arduino 5V pin can supply. It requires a dedicated 6.6V regulated supply from a separate LM2596 buck converter (not the Arduino rail), with a 1000µF electrolytic capacitor across its power terminals to absorb current spikes. Without this, the module resets during transmission.
