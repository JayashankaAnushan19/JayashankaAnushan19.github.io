AI Tools Used

Claude (Anthropic) — used as a coding and writing assistant across two distinct parts of this project:

AreaHow AI was used
Literature ReviewUsed for research assistance and drafting support during the early Literature Review phase (per field-book notes, March 2026). All sources were selected, read, and verified by the author; AI assistance was used for drafting and organisation, not for generating claims or citations independently.
This companion websiteUsed to build and maintain the CARA documentation site itself — page structure, styling, the blog write-ups (based on the author's field-book notes and photos), image organisation, and technical documentation formatting (requirements tables, architecture diagrams, this appendix section).
Firmware & codeUsed for code review, debugging assistance, and documentation of the Arduino firmware and related scripts. All hardware design, wiring, testing, and core logic decisions were made by the author; AI was used to help debug, document, and clean up code — not to originate the system design.

What Was Not AI-Generated

  • All hardware design, assembly, wiring, and physical testing
  • All research objectives, requirements, and architectural decisions
  • All experimental results and data collected during testing
  • Final review and verification of all content published on this site and in the thesis document

Declaration

The author takes full responsibility for the accuracy and integrity of all work presented in this project, regardless of which tools assisted in its preparation.
