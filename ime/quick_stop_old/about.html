Who we are

A corner shop that knows your name

Quick Stop News & Booze on Linden Drive is a Lostock Hall regular — newsagent, off-licence and convenience store in one, with a friendly counter and a proper range of drinks, snacks and everyday bits.

Quick Stop Essentials on Station Road in Bamber Bridge is our Bamber Bridge branch: everyday groceries and household essentials, open late for whenever you run out of something.

Customers tell us the same things again and again — helpful staff, fair prices, great choice. So that's what we keep working on.
