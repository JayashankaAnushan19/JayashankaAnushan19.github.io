How to publish your changes

  1. Make your changes — add, edit or delete products, set offers, add photos. They're saved as a draft in this browser, so you can close the page and carry on later.
  2. Publish — press Publish to website (Chrome or Edge). The first time, pick your website folder (the one containing index.html). The page then writes the new js/data.js and your photos into images/products/ for you, and keeps a backup of the old file in admin/backups/.
  3. No Chrome/Edge? Press Download update (ZIP), unzip it, and copy the js and images folders over your website folder, choosing "replace".
  4. Upload to your host — upload the website files as usual, but do not upload the admin folder. That keeps this page private.

Good to know

  • Photos are automatically resized, compressed and renamed to something tidy like images/products/news-booze-carlsberg-lager.jpg.
  • Offers with an "until" date switch themselves off on the website — no need to come back and remove them.
  • Deleting a product doesn't delete its photo file, so nothing is lost by accident.
  • Product IDs are kept the same when you edit, so customers' saved baskets keep working.
