Plant Mixing & Compatibility Guide
Golden rule: only mix plants with matching water schedules
The plant that drowns first will ruin the pot and your reputation. Group by watering needs first, then consider light. Start with single-species pots for months 1–3. Introduce combinations only after you understand each plant's behaviour in your specific greenhouse environment.

⚠️ UAE-specific watering reminder
In the Sajja Industrial warehouse environment, your AC will keep the greenhouse at 22–26°C but will also dry the air. Monitor soil moisture with your finger — the top 2–3 cm should be completely dry before watering drought-tolerant plants. In summer months (June–September), the AC runs harder and soil may dry slightly faster. Adjust watering frequency by ±2 days based on observation, not a fixed calendar.

Environmental Systems — Critical for UAE
Cooling / AC1.5 ton split AC minimum — Sajja summer = 45°C outside
Humidity controlPortable dehumidifier 20–30L/day capacity
Ventilation2× inline fans for air circulation — prevent mold
Grow lightsFull-spectrum LED bars — 4× 50W strips minimum
TimersSmart timer for lights — 12–14h cycle
Most critical issue — moisture vs. warehouse
Your greenhouse will be humid inside (60–70%). The Sajja warehouse outside is hot and dry. The polycarbonate skin must create a complete seal or condensation will drip on your warehouse floor, creating mold risk and structural damage. Seal every joint with silicone. This is the #1 failure point for indoor greenhouses in UAE.

Zero to Market — 18-Month Plan
Month 1–2
Build & Plant
Construct greenhouse frame, install AC + lights + drainage. Source 100–150 starter plants (Snake Plant, ZZ, Pothos, Spider Plant). Set up organic soil beds. Do not sell yet.
Month 2–3
Environment Calibration
Run the greenhouse for 4–6 weeks with no product pressure. Dial in temperature (22–26°C), humidity (55–65%), light cycles. Observe plant health. Fix problems before you have customers.
Month 3
Brand & Content Launch
Name your brand. Build Instagram. Start documenting the greenhouse — behind-the-scenes, plant care tips, organic process. Post daily. Build 500+ followers before selling.
Month 4
First Sales — Soft Launch
Pot your first batch (20–30 pots). Sell through Instagram DM and WhatsApp. Offer free delivery to 5 offices in Sharjah in exchange for photos and reviews. Create your first B2B pitch deck.
Month 5–6
B2B Push — Offices & Interior Designers
Target interior designers in Sharjah and Dubai. Offer a "Greenhouse Origins" certificate with each pot. Pitch office plant packages (5–10 pots, quarterly replacement service).
Month 6–9
Expand Stock & Premium Products
Add Monstera, Rubber Plant, Peace Lily, Calathea, Areca Palm. Introduce premium combination pots (AED 250–500). Add plant care subscription for offices — monthly visit + care AED 200–400/client.
Month 9–12
Online Store + Delivery Coverage
Launch Shopify or Instagram shop. Cover Sharjah + Dubai delivery. Target gifting season (Ramadan, Diwali, New Year). Corporate gifting is huge in UAE — organic plant pots as branded gifts.
Month 12–18
Scale or Replicate
By month 12 you'll know what sells, margins, and the demand ceiling. Options: add a second greenhouse unit, hire part-time staff, or begin supplying wholesale to a garden centre.
Key Risks & Mitigation
AC failure in summer — plants die within 6 hours at 45°CCritical
Mitigation: Keep a backup portable AC unit. Set a temperature alarm on your phone via a smart plug with temperature sensor (AED 30).
Humidity leaking to warehouse — mold and corrosionCritical
Mitigation: Silicone seal every joint. Run dehumidifier continuously. Check warehouse walls monthly for moisture signs.
Slow sales in months 1–4 — cash pressureMedium
Mitigation: Start Instagram 2 months before selling. Build audience first. Reserve 2 months operating costs (AED 6,000) before launch.
Plant disease spreading in enclosed spaceMedium
Mitigation: Quarantine new plant arrivals for 2 weeks before entering greenhouse. Inspect weekly. Use neem oil (organic) as preventive spray.
Power outage — DEWA disruptionsLow–Medium
Mitigation: UAE grid is reliable, but keep a UPS for fans and sensors. A small generator (AED 1,500–2,000) protects your entire stock investment.
Organic certification — no formal claim without certStrategic
Mitigation: In Phase 1 use "naturally grown" or "chemical-free" language. Apply for Sharjah Municipality or UAE Ministry of Climate Change registration in Year 2.
