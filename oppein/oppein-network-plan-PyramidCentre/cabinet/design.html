DES-03

Engineering checks

ResultItemWhy
CheckSwitch shelf width (U5)Switch-01 + Switch-02 + 2 injectors side by side take about 45 of 45 cm. If the real injectors are bigger, stand them beside the router on the U6 shelf (≈ 21 cm free there).
FitsSwitch shelf height (U5)Only 4.2 cm under the router shelf; the tallest unit is ≈ 3.5 cm. Plug the cords in from the front and route them down to the cable manager.
FitsRouter + ONT stack (U6–U10)Router ≈ 4 cm + ONT ≈ 3 cm on top = 7 cm in a 22 cm zone (U6–U10). Room for router antennas up to ≈ 18 cm; don't put anything heavy on the ONT.
CheckDVR and camera supply (U11)The DVR (≈ 4.5 cm) and the 12 V supply box (≈ 5 cm) are taller than one shelf space (4.2 cm), so they rise into U12 — keep U12 free (a front blank panel is fine).
CheckDepthThe plan sheet does not give the cabinet depth: choose ≥ 450 mm. The deepest unit is the DVR (≈ 22 cm) plus ≈ 5 cm for plugs; use shelves ≈ 25–35 cm deep and keep the back clear for cables.
FitsPower sockets8 plugs (ONT, router, 2 switches, 2 PoE injectors, DVR, camera supply) on two 6-way PDUs = 12 sockets, 4 spare. The cabinet needs one 13 A wall socket per PDU, or one PDU fed through the UPS.
CheckHeatAbout 110 W in a closed cabinet (ONT 10 W, Router 15 W, Switch-01 + Switch-02 10 W, 2 PoE injectors (AP load + losses) 30 W, DVR + disk 15 W, 12 V camera supply (4 cameras + losses) 30 W). Fit the roof fan (SYS-03 BOQ D.1) and close the free U with blank panels so the air passes through the kit.
FitsCable entry for 18 cables14 Cat6 (Ø6 mm) + 4 siamese RG59 + power (≈ 7 × 11 mm) ≈ 6 cm² of cable: enter through the top cable entry from the ceiling conduit with a brush grommet. Coax goes straight to the DVR, not via the patch panel.
NoRack UPSA 1–1.5 kVA rack UPS takes 2U, is ≈ 40–50 cm deep and weighs 15–25 kg — too much for a 12U wall cabinet. Use a tower UPS beside or under the cabinet (SYS-03 BOQ C.1).
CheckWall fixingCabinet ≈ 15–20 kg plus ≈ 8–10 kg of equipment: fix to masonry with heavy-duty anchors, not to plasterboard alone.
DES-04

Earlier concept — custom laminate module (superseded)

Click an image to enlarge.

Not used: before the plan sheet, a custom wall-hung laminate box (80 × 40 × 35 cm inside, 18 mm board, two shelves, no door) was sketched. It has no 19″ rails, so the patch panel, cable manager and PDUs would not mount, and a 40 cm height is only ≈ 9U. The 12U cabinet replaces it. The drawings are kept for reference.
