DOC 01 · CABINET DESIGN

Cabinet design

The proposed cabinet is a custom wall-hung laminate module fixed above the desk, not a standard 19″ rack: 80 × 40 × 35 cm inside, 18 mm board, two shelves, a back cable inlet, and top and bottom ventilation. This page collects the sample drawings, the dimensions read from them, and how the equipment would fit.

DES-03

How the equipment would sit (to scale)

Front view, 1 cm = 1 grid unit. Warm kit (router, ONT) goes in the top bay right under the vent slot; the switches and PoE injectors in the middle bay; the patch panel on a 1U wall bracket and the PDU in the bottom bay. The Ø10 cm inlet at the back top-left takes every cable from the ceiling conduit.

DES-04

Engineering checks

ResultItemWhy
FitsHeight budget40 cm inside ≈ 9U, the same height as the 9U rack plan in SYS-01 — but there are no 19″ rails, so everything sits on shelves or brackets.
Fits19″ patch panel and PDUBoth are 48 cm wide; the 80 cm width leaves 32 cm. The panel needs a 1U wall-mount bracket on the back board.
FitsCable inlet for 18 cablesØ10 cm ≈ 79 cm². 14 Cat6 (Ø6 mm ≈ 0.28 cm²) + 4 siamese RG59 + power (≈ 7 × 11 mm ≈ 0.6 cm²) use about 6 cm² — plenty of room. Fit a brush grommet.
CheckShelf depth21 cm: typical router and desktop switches are 10–20 cm deep, but allow ~5 cm for the plugs at the back. Measure the router before ordering.
CheckRouter antennasTop bay ≈ 14 cm: fold the antennas sideways or pick a router with internal antennas. Wi-Fi from inside a wooden box is weaker — the APs carry the office Wi-Fi.
CheckHeatAbout 115 W in the cabinet (ONT 10 W, Router 15 W, SW0 + SW1 + SW2 15 W, 2 PoE injectors (AP load + losses) 30 W, DVR + disk 15 W, 12 V camera supply (4 cameras + losses) 30 W). The top slot + lower gap make a chimney, but in Dubai add a quiet extract fan on the top slot and keep 5 cm free behind the kit.
CheckOpen frontHinge-free means no door: fine in a locked IT Room; otherwise add a lockable front panel so cables and ports cannot be pulled.
CheckCCTV recorder + powerA compact 4-channel DVR (about 26 cm wide, 5 cm high, 22 cm deep) fits the right side of the top bay, and the 12 V camera supply the middle bay; check both depths against the 21 cm shelf. Coax goes straight to the DVR, not via the patch panel.
NoRack UPSA 1–1.5 kVA rack UPS (48 × 9 × 40 cm) is deeper than the cabinet and too heavy for a wall-hung board box. Use a tower UPS under the desk.
CheckWall fixingBoard cabinet plus ~10–15 kg of equipment: fix to masonry with heavy-duty anchors, not to plasterboard alone.
