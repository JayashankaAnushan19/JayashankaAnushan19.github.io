REC-03

Security

  • The analog cameras are not network devices — they cannot be reached or hacked over the network. The DVR is the only CCTV device on the LAN.
  • Change the DVR default password; one admin account for IT, view-only accounts for staff.
  • Remote viewing through the maker's app with two-factor login, or a VPN — no open router ports.
  • Keep the DVR firmware up to date; turn off UPnP and unused services.
  • Export clips only on request, to an encrypted USB drive; keep an export log.
