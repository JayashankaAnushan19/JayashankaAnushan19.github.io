CAM-03

How lengths are worked out

Pull length = ( ceiling route + 1.5 m cabinet drop + 0.6 m camera drop ) × 1.1, rounded up

At the cabinet each siamese cable splits: the coax gets a BNC connector into the DVR input, the power pair goes to its fused output on the 12 V supply. At the camera: BNC on the coax, DC pigtail on the power pair. Total 51 m — order 1 × 100 m roll.
