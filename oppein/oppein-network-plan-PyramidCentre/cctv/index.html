Overview

The camera system for Office 114: 4 HD analog cameras recorded by a DVR in the shared cabinet. Each camera has one siamese cable (coax for video + a power pair) back to the cabinet. One camera covers the Designers strip, two face each other across the Showroom, and one covers the kitchen.

Design choices

Coverage

  • CAM1 on the Designers ceiling at the printer looks down the whole strip, so every desk and the printer are in view.
  • CAM2 and CAM3 face each other across the Showroom: each one sees the other's blind spot below it, and together they cover the entrance.
  • CAM4 covers the kitchen from the corner nearest the cabinet — the shortest camera cable.

System

  • One siamese cable per camera (RG59 coax + 2 × 0.75 mm² power in one jacket), home-run to the cabinet: CAM1 and CAM3 through the ceiling conduit to mark 2, CAM2 and CAM4 straight across the ceiling.
  • Video: coax → DVR BNC input. Power: power pair → 12 V DC CCTV power supply (one fused output per camera). Both sit in the cabinet.
  • Analog cameras are not on any network. Only the DVR joins the office LAN (patch cord to SW0-P8) for phone-app viewing.
  • Camera cables do not use the network patch panel or the Cat6 cable.
CCTV-04

To confirm

  • Exact camera spots on site (ceiling grid, lights, sprinklers) and the kitchen corner.
  • Camera and DVR must use the same analog standard (AHD / TVI / CVI) — buy them as a set.
  • Kitchen camera: agree with management that staff areas may be recorded, and put up notice signs.
  • Dubai rules: CCTV in many commercial premises falls under SIRA requirements — check whether this office does, and use an approved installer if so.
  • Retention period wanted (the 2 TB disk gives ≈ 20 days).
  • The DVR takes the last spare port on SW0 (P8).
