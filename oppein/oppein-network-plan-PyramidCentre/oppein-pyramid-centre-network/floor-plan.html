PLAN-02

Conduit and casing route

Cables for the Design area leave the top of the cabinet into a PVC conduit in the ceiling and run straight to mark 2 (C-01, C-12). At mark 2 they come down behind the wall to skirting level and continue in wall casing along the desk wall to desks 3, 4, 5 and 6. AP2 (Showroom) leaves the casing at mark 2, goes back up and crosses the ceiling in its own conduit. AP1 has a casing up the wall to the ceiling. The 4 IT Room PCs stay inside the IT Room in casing along its walls.
