PLAN-02

Tray route and junctions

All cables leave the cabinet, rise to ceiling level and exit the IT Room above the door. A single ceiling tray runs diagonally across the Showroom to the Design area. Each cable leaves the tray at the junction closest to its outlet, then drops down the wall (desks) or goes to a ceiling box (APs). Tray length: 12.9 m (15 m ordered).
