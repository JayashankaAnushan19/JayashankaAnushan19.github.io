Power, cooling and access

  • Power: one dedicated 230 V socket next to the cabinet feeding the PDU. A UPS is recommended (open item D-05).
  • Cooling: fan on top of the cabinet; keep the side vents clear. U2 can take a fan tray if the top fan is not possible.
  • Mounting: fix to a solid wall at a height where the top of the cabinet is below the ceiling tray exit, so cables enter through the brush panel.
  • Access: lockable glass door; key kept by IT. Leave about 600 mm clear in front for working.
  • Patch cords: one colour per device type makes faults easier to trace: blue for PCs, yellow for APs, green for printer and server, red for the router uplink.
