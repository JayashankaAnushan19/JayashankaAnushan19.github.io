Power, cooling and access

  • Power: a dedicated 230 V socket next to the cabinet for the two 6-way PDUs (8 plugs in use, 4 spare). A tower UPS beside the cabinet is recommended (open item D-05).
  • Cooling: fan on top of the cabinet; keep the side vents clear and close the spare slots with blank panels.
  • Mounting: fix to a solid wall; the cables come down from the ceiling conduit and the IT Room casing and enter through the top cable entry (brush grommet).
  • Access: lockable glass door; key kept by IT. Leave about 600 mm clear in front for working.
  • Patch cords: one colour per use makes faults easier to trace: red for router-to-switch cords, blue for PCs, yellow for AP / injector cords, pink RJ11–RJ45 cord for the phone line.
