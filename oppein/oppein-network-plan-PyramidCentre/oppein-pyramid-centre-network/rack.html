Power, cooling and access

  • Power: one dedicated 230 V socket next to the cabinet feeding the PDU. A UPS is recommended (open item D-05).
  • Cooling: fan on top of the cabinet; keep the side vents clear and close the spare slots with blank panels.
  • Mounting: fix to a solid wall; the casing comes down beside the cabinet and the cables enter through the brush panel.
  • Access: lockable glass door; key kept by IT. Leave about 600 mm clear in front for working.
  • Patch cords: one colour per use makes faults easier to trace: red for the uplink to SW1, blue for PCs, pink RJ11–RJ45 cord for the phone line. Use the same colours at the switch desks (yellow for AP / injector cords).
