Overview

Cat6 structured cabling and the local network for Office 14 at Pyramid Centre. Every cable is a home run from one 16U cabinet in the IT Room to a single outlet. The runs serve 8 PCs, a printer, a mobile point, and 2 Wi-Fi access points. Everything needed to approve, buy and install it is in these documents.

SCOPE

Scope of work

Draft scope based on the cabling plan. To be agreed before work starts.

In scope

  • Supply and mount a 19″ 16U wall cabinet in the IT Room
  • Ceiling cable tray from the IT Room door to the Design area
  • 12 Cat6 home runs, terminated on a 24-port patch panel and RJ45 outlets
  • Labelling of every cable at both ends, patch panel and faceplate
  • Testing of every link and a written test record
  • Fitting and configuring the PoE switch, router shelf and access points

Not in scope (unless agreed)

  • ISP fibre to the building and the ONT itself (ISP)
  • Electrical works: new power sockets, the cabinet supply circuit
  • PC, printer and software set-up
  • CCTV, access control, telephone system
  • Ceiling repair or painting after the work
