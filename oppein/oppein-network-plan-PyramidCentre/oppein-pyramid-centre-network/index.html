DOC 00 · OVERVIEW

Overview

The local network and Cat6 cabling for Office 14 at Pyramid Centre. The ONT, router and a rack switch (SW0) sit in a cabinet in the IT Room and serve the 4 wired IT Room PCs. One uplink cable runs in wall casing to a 5-port switch (SW1) at desk 3, which feeds an 8-port switch (SW2) at desk 5. A separate phone line runs from the ONT to the mobile at desk 5. Between them they connect 9 PCs, a printer, a mobile point and 2 Wi-Fi access points. Everything needed to approve, buy and install it is in these documents.

SCOPE

Scope of work

Draft scope based on the cabling plan. To be agreed before work starts.

In scope

  • 19″ 9U wall cabinet in the IT Room for the ONT, router and an 8-port switch (SW0), with a small patch panel and 4U kept free
  • PVC conduit in the ceiling from the cabinet to mark 2 (≈ 16 m), then wall casing to every desk and up to AP1 (≈ 20 m)
  • 12 Cat6 runs (108 m), terminated in RJ45 surface boxes and on the cabinet patch panel
  • Fixing SW1 (5-port) and SW2 (8-port) at desks 3 and 5, with PoE injectors for AP1 and AP2
  • A separate phone line (C-12) from the ONT phone port to the mobile at desk 5
  • Labelling of every cable at both ends, panel port and surface box
  • Testing of every link, a written test record, and configuring the router and access points

Not in scope (unless agreed)

  • ISP fibre to the building and the ONT itself (ISP)
  • Electrical works: new power sockets, the cabinet supply circuit
  • PC, printer and software set-up
  • CCTV, access control, telephone system
  • Painting or making good the walls after the casing is fixed
