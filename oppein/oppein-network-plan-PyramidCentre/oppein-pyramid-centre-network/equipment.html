Equipment & buying guide

EQ-01

Cat6 cable

BOQ quantity: 1 box

Function

The permanent link between the router or a switch and each device, laid in the casing. Each run carries 1 Gbps data, and on the two AP runs it also carries up to 30 W of PoE power from the injector.

Must have

  • Cat6 U/UTP, 23 AWG
  • Solid bare copper (BC), never CCA
  • LSZH jacket for indoor ceilings
  • 305 m box (pull-box)
Recommended OPTERNET Cat6 23 AWG UTP, LSZH, 100 % bare copper, 305 m Amazon.aeAED 342 View listing ↗ Brand D-Link Cat6 UTP cable roll, 305 m noon UAEAED 479.99 View listing ↗ Premium Belden 7965E Cat6 UTP, 23 AWG solid bare copper, 305 m Amazon.aecheck listing View listing ↗ Premium Schneider Cat6 UTP cable, 305 m noon UAEAED 934.32 View listing ↗
Avoid: Boxes marked "CCA" (copper-clad aluminium), e.g. many AED 170 – 300 listings. CCA has about 55 % more resistance, so PoE devices can brown out. It also fails Cat6 certification and is not allowed by the standard.
EQ-02

Switches (SW0 rack, SW1 5-port, SW2 8-port)

BOQ quantity: 1 pc + 1 pc + 1 pc

Function

Plain unmanaged Gigabit switches, all on one shelf in the cabinet. The router feeds SW0 (new 8-port); SW0 feeds the existing SW1 (5-port) and SW2 (8-port) with short patch cords. Every device cable lands on the patch panel and is patched to its switch port. None of them give PoE, so the APs use injectors.

Must have

  • Gigabit (10/100/1000) on every port
  • All three fit side by side on one 1U shelf (about 440 mm wide in total)
  • Metal case preferred (cabinet heat)
  • Each power adapter on the cabinet PDU
  • Same make for both switches is easiest

Normally supplied by the installer at trade price. See the BOQ for the allowance.

Avoid: Fast Ethernet (10/100) switches: they cap every PC behind them at 100 Mbps. Do not chain a third switch behind SW2; add ports by replacing a switch instead.
EQ-03

PoE injectors

BOQ quantity: 2 pc

Function

A small box in the cabinet between the switch and the AP cable. It adds power to the data cable so the AP needs no socket at the ceiling: data in from the switch, data + power out to the AP.

Must have

  • 802.3at (PoE+, 30 W), or whatever the AP datasheet asks for
  • Gigabit
  • Powered from the cabinet PDU, on the shelf next to the router

Normally supplied by the installer at trade price. See the BOQ for the allowance.

Avoid: Passive 24 V injectors with a standard 802.3af/at AP (and the reverse). Match the AP's PoE type exactly. Many UniFi APs ship with their own PoE adapter; check the box before buying.
EQ-04

Wi-Fi access points

BOQ quantity: 2 pc

Function

Ceiling-mounted radios that give wireless access to laptops, phones and tablets. AP1 covers the Showroom and AP2 the Design area. Each one is powered through its cable by a PoE injector in the cabinet (AP1 on SW1, AP2 on SW2), and both are managed from one app or controller.

Must have

  • Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax), dual band
  • PoE powered, ceiling mount kit
  • Same brand for all APs (seamless roaming)
  • Guest SSID with client isolation

EQ-05

Cabinet & patch panel

BOQ quantity: 1 pc + 1 pc + 1 pc + 1 pc + 4 pc

Function

The cabinet in the IT Room protects the ONT and router and keeps them locked. The 14 cables that start there end on a small patch panel, so changes are made with patch cords, not by re-terminating cables.

Must have

  • 19″ 9U wall-mount (holds everything + 4U spare), glass door, lock
  • ≥ 450 mm deep (600 mm if the router is large)
  • Fan + vented sides
  • 24-port Cat6 patch panel (keystone or loaded)
Recommended D-Link 24-port Cat6 UTP loaded patch panel (NPP-C61BLK241) noon UAEAED 141.55 View listing ↗ Alternative VENTION Cat6 UTP 24-port keystone patch panel (KGCB0) noon UAEAED 123.55 View listing ↗
Avoid: FTP / STP (shielded) patch panels with UTP cable. Shielding only works if the whole system is shielded and earthed.
EQ-06

Outlets: keystones & surface boxes

BOQ quantity: 28 pc + 14 pc

Function

The socket at each end of a run. The keystone jack terminates the Cat6 cable; the surface box holds it at the end of the casing, so no wall box is needed. It must be Cat6-rated, or the whole link drops to the lower grade.

Must have

  • Cat6 keystone, T568B
  • Surface-mount box (1-port at devices, 2-port at switches) with label window
  • Same brand as cable if possible

Avoid: Cat5e keystones on Cat6 cable. They cap the link at Cat5e.
EQ-07

Patch cords

BOQ quantity: 18 pc + 11 pc + 2 pc + 1 pc + 2 pc + 4 pc

Function

Short, flexible factory-made leads: 0.5 m in the cabinet (panel → switch, switch → switch, switch → injector), 1 m from each injector to the panel, and 2 m at the desk (surface box → device).

Must have

  • Cat6, factory moulded
  • Stranded copper
  • Colour by device type (see DOC 03)

EQ-08

Upgrade option: one 16-port PoE+ switch

BOQ quantity: 1 pc

Function

Optional (BOQ F.3, decision D-03). One switch in place of SW1 + SW2 + the injectors: it powers the APs over the cable, has spare ports for new desks, and a smart-managed model can separate guest Wi-Fi with a VLAN.

Must have

  • 16 × 1 GbE PoE+ (802.3at, 30 W / port)
  • PoE budget ≥ 150 W (2 PoE devices + growth)
  • Rackmount ears (fits the spare 4U in the cabinet)
  • Smart-managed (VLAN) preferred

EQ-09

UPS (optional)

BOQ quantity: 1 pc

Function

Battery backup for the cabinet. During a power cut it keeps internet, Wi-Fi and the switch alive for several minutes. It also cleans spikes, which protects the equipment.

Must have

  • 1000 VA line-interactive
  • Pure sine wave
  • 230 V model (not 120 V)
  • Rack or tower

EQ-10

Cable casing (PVC trunking) & labelling

BOQ quantity: 7 length + 5 length + 6 length + 1 lot + 1 lot + 28 pc + 1 lot

Function

A plastic channel fixed to the wall with a snap-on lid; the cables lie inside it. Here the cables reach mark 2 through a PVC conduit in the ceiling, come down behind the wall, and the casing carries them along the desk wall to each desk and up the wall to AP1. Labels make every cable traceable.

Must have

  • 25 × 16 mm for up to 4 Cat6, 40 × 25 mm for up to 10–12
  • Screw-fix every 500 mm (adhesive alone falls off)
  • Proper corner and tee fittings, no tight folds
  • Separate (or divided) casing from power, ≥ 300 mm apart
  • Printed labels at both ends

Normally supplied by the installer at trade price. See the BOQ for the allowance.

Avoid: Over-filling the casing (keep 30–40 % free for a later cable), cutting the lid short at corners, and mixing data with mains cables in one channel.
EQ-11

Labour & testing

BOQ quantity: 14 run + 21 m + 16 m + 1 lot + 14 run + 1 lot + 1 lot + 14 run

Function

Installation, termination, testing, configuration and handover by an installer. Ask for a line-by-line price against this BOQ.

Must have

  • Installer used to surface casing work
  • Cable tester (wire map + length) at minimum
  • Certification tester if a warranty is required

Normally supplied by the installer at trade price. See the BOQ for the allowance.

Avoid: Quotes that do not name the cable brand or say whether testing is included.
