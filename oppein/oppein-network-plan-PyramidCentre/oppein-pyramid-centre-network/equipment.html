EQ-01

Cat6 cable

BOQ quantity: 1 box

Function

The permanent link between the cabinet and each outlet. Each run carries 1 Gbps data, and on the AP and mobile runs it also carries up to 30 W of PoE power.

Must have

  • Cat6 U/UTP, 23 AWG
  • Solid bare copper (BC), never CCA
  • LSZH jacket for indoor ceilings
  • 305 m box (pull-box)
Recommended OPTERNET Cat6 23 AWG UTP, LSZH, 100 % bare copper, 305 m Amazon.aeAED 342 View listing ↗ Brand D-Link Cat6 UTP cable roll, 305 m noon UAEAED 479.99 View listing ↗ Premium Belden 7965E Cat6 UTP, 23 AWG solid bare copper, 305 m Amazon.aecheck listing View listing ↗ Premium Schneider Cat6 UTP cable, 305 m noon UAEAED 934.32 View listing ↗
Avoid: Boxes marked "CCA" (copper-clad aluminium), e.g. many AED 170 – 300 listings. CCA has about 55 % more resistance, so PoE devices can brown out. It also fails Cat6 certification and is not allowed by the standard.
EQ-02

PoE+ switch

BOQ quantity: 1 pc

Function

The centre of the network. Every cable ends on a switch port. It powers the access points and the mobile point through the cable (PoE), so they need no power socket. A smart-managed model can separate guest Wi-Fi with a VLAN.

Must have

  • 16 × 1 GbE PoE+ (802.3at, 30 W / port)
  • PoE budget ≥ 150 W (3 PoE devices + growth)
  • Rackmount ears for the 16U cabinet
  • Smart-managed (VLAN) preferred

EQ-03

Wi-Fi access points

BOQ quantity: 2 pc

Function

Ceiling-mounted radios that give wireless access to laptops, phones and tablets. AP1 covers the Showroom and AP2 the Design area. They run on PoE from the switch and use one app or controller.

Must have

  • Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax), dual band
  • PoE powered, ceiling mount kit
  • Same brand for all APs (seamless roaming)
  • Guest SSID with client isolation
Recommended Ubiquiti UniFi 6 Lite (U6-Lite) — 2×2 Wi-Fi 6, PoE Amazon.aeAED 400 – 555 View listing ↗ Step up Ubiquiti UniFi U6+ — Wi-Fi 6, 300+ clients, ceiling/wall kit noon UAEcheck listing View listing ↗ Store Ubiquiti store on noon UAE (U6 Mesh, U6-IW and others) noon UAEAED 729 – 899 View listing ↗
Avoid: Buying a PoE injector is not needed. The PoE+ switch powers the APs. Check that the listing is the international (not "-US") model.
EQ-04

Cabinet & patch panel

BOQ quantity: 1 pc + 1 pc + 2 pc + 1 pc + 1 pc + 1 pc + 4 pc

Function

The cabinet protects and ventilates the equipment and keeps it locked. The patch panel is where all 12 cables end permanently, so moves are done with patch cords, not by re-terminating cables.

Must have

  • 19″ 16U wall-mount, glass door, lock
  • ≥ 450 mm deep (600 mm if the router is large)
  • Fan + vented sides
  • Loaded 24-port Cat6 UTP patch panel

EQ-05

Outlets: keystones & faceplates

BOQ quantity: 11 pc + 9 pc + 2 pc

Function

The socket the user plugs into. The keystone jack terminates the Cat6 cable; the faceplate holds it in the wall box. It must be Cat6-rated, or the whole link drops to the lower grade.

Must have

  • Cat6 keystone, T568B
  • UK-size single faceplate (86 mm) with label window
  • Same brand as cable if possible
Recommended CableCreation Cat6 keystone jacks, 10-pack (UL listed) Amazon.aecheck listing View listing ↗ Kit LEENUE Cat6 single faceplate with keystone jack Amazon.aecheck listing View listing ↗ Kit VCELINK Cat6 single wall socket with keystone Amazon.aecheck listing View listing ↗

EQ-06

Patch cords

BOQ quantity: 13 pc + 9 pc + 4 pc

Function

Short, flexible factory-made leads: 0.5 m in the cabinet (patch panel → switch) and 2 m at the desk (outlet → device).

Must have

  • Cat6, factory moulded
  • Stranded copper
  • Colour by device type (see DOC 03)

EQ-07

UPS (optional)

BOQ quantity: 1 pc

Function

Battery backup for the cabinet. During a power cut it keeps internet, Wi-Fi and the switch alive for several minutes. It also cleans spikes, which protects the equipment.

Must have

  • 1000 VA line-interactive
  • Pure sine wave
  • 230 V model (not 120 V)
  • Rack or tower

EQ-08

Containment & labelling

BOQ quantity: 15 m + 27 m + 24 pc + 1 lot

Function

Tray, J-hooks and trunking carry the cables safely. Labels make every cable traceable. Containment is usually supplied by the installer at trade price.

Must have

  • Ceiling tray or J-hooks every 1.2 – 1.5 m
  • PVC trunking for drops
  • Printed labels both ends

Normally supplied by the installer at trade price. See the BOQ for the allowance.

Avoid: Cable ties pulled tight, and cables laid directly on ceiling tiles.
EQ-09

Labour & testing

BOQ quantity: 12 point + 42 m + 1 lot + 12 point + 1 lot + 1 lot + 12 point

Function

Installation, termination, testing, configuration and handover by an installer. Ask for a line-by-line price against this BOQ.

Must have

  • Experienced structured-cabling installer
  • Cable tester (wire map + length) at minimum
  • Certification tester if a warranty is required

Normally supplied by the installer at trade price. See the BOQ for the allowance.

Avoid: Quotes that do not name the cable brand or say whether testing is included.
