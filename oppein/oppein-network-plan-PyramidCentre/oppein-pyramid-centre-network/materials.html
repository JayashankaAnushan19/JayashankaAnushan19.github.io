Notes & conditions

  1. Budget estimate for planning and approval. Rates are UAE market estimates (17 Oct 2026) and are replaced by supplier quotations before ordering.
  2. Quantities follow the Office 14 cable schedule: 12 Cat6 home runs, 229 m, 11 outlets. To be confirmed after the site survey.
  3. Excluded: ISP fibre and ONT, electrical works and sockets, ceiling repair or painting, PCs, printers and software licences.
  4. Optional items (section F) are included only where ticked. Contingency covers site variations.
  5. All cable and termination to be Cat6 solid bare copper; CCA cable is not accepted.
