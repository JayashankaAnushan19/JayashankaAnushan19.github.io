Notes & conditions

  1. Budget estimate for planning and approval. Rates are UAE market estimates (17 Oct 2026) and are replaced by supplier quotations before ordering.
  2. Quantities follow the Office 14 cable schedule: 14 Cat6 runs, 235 m, ≈ 20 m of wall casing. To be confirmed after the site survey.
  3. SW1 (5-port) and SW2 (8-port) are existing units and are priced at zero. SW0 (cabinet) is new.
  4. Excluded: ISP fibre and ONT, electrical works and sockets, painting or making good, PCs, printers and software licences.
  5. Optional items (section F) are included only where ticked. Contingency covers site variations.
  6. All cable and termination to be Cat6 solid bare copper; CCA cable is not accepted.
