DOC 02 · TOPOLOGY & PORTS

Topology & ports

How the network fits together: the ISP line comes into the router, the router feeds the PoE switch, and each switch port is patched to the matching patch-panel port and cable.

Pyramid Centre · Office 14Rev A · 2026-10-16Jayashanka · OPPEIN IT
NET-01

Logical topology

ISP fibre → ONT / router → PoE switch → patch panel → wall or ceiling outlet → device. Port numbers are the same on the switch, the patch panel and the cable ID.

NET-02

Port map

Switch port = patch-panel port = cable number. Switch ports 14 and 15 and 16 are spare. Patch-panel ports PP-17 to PP-24 have no switch port and stay unpatched.
