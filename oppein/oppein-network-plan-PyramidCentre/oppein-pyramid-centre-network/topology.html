DOC 02 · TOPOLOGY & PORTS

Topology & ports

How the network fits together: the ISP line comes into the ONT and router in the cabinet. The router feeds the cabinet switch SW0 (IT Room PCs), one uplink cable feeds SW1 at desk 3, and SW1 feeds SW2 at desk 5. The ONT phone port has its own line to desk 5.

Pyramid Centre · Office 14Rev C · 2026-10-22Jayashanka · OPPEIN IT
NET-01

Logical topology

ISP fibre → ONT → router → SW0 (cabinet) → (C-01) → SW1 → (C-02) → SW2. Dotted lines are patch cords with no cable run; the dashed pink line is the phone line. The APs get power from a PoE injector beside their switch.

Wi-Fi

OPPEIN-OfficeStaff laptops and phonesWPA2/WPA3-Personal, strong passphrase
OPPEIN-GuestVisitors and showroom clientsSeparate passphrase, client isolation on the AP, internet only

The switches are unmanaged (no VLANs), so guest isolation is done by the APs' guest-network setting. The same SSIDs go on all access points so devices roam between them. Use non-overlapping 2.4 GHz channels (1 / 6 / 11) and auto channel on 5 GHz. After installation, walk the office with a Wi-Fi analyser to confirm coverage (see DOC 09 · Testing & handover).
