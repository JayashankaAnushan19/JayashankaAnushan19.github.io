DOC 02 · TOPOLOGY & PORTS

Topology & ports

How the network fits together: the ISP line comes into the ONT and router in the cabinet. The router feeds Switch-02 and Switch-01 directly with patch cords; every device cable starts on the cabinet patch panel and is patched to its switch or router port. The ONT phone port has its own line to desk 5. The mobile's data cable is pulled but not patched (no free port).

NET-01

Logical topology

ISP fibre → ONT → router → Switch-02 (L1) and Switch-01 (L2), all patched inside the cabinet. Every device has its own cable from the cabinet; the dashed pink line is the phone line. The APs get power from a PoE injector beside their switch.

The switches are unmanaged (no VLANs), so guest isolation is done by the APs' guest-network setting. The same SSIDs go on all access points so devices roam between them. Use non-overlapping 2.4 GHz channels (1 / 6 / 11) and auto channel on 5 GHz. After installation, walk the office with a Wi-Fi analyser to confirm coverage (see DOC 09 · Testing & handover).
