CAB-02

How lengths are worked out

Pull length = ( tray route + 2.0 m cabinet drop + outlet drop ) × 1.10, rounded up to the next metre

PartAllowance
Tray routeMeasured on the plan from the cabinet, along the tray, to the junction, then straight to the outlet
Cabinet drop2.0 m: up the wall from the cabinet to ceiling level, plus the dressing loop in the cabinet
Outlet drop2.5 m wall outlet (ceiling 2.8 m down to 300 mm) · 0.6 m ceiling AP · 0.5 m direct in IT Room
Slack10 % for routing around obstacles and termination
The longest run is C-04 at 24 m, well inside the 90 m Cat6 permanent-link limit. Cut lengths on site from the real route, not from this table; this table is for ordering and checking.
