DOC 08 · INSTALLATION METHOD

Installation method

Rules for the installer and the order of work. Everything runs in surface casing on the walls, so no ceiling access is needed except at the two AP positions.

Pyramid Centre · Office 14Rev D · 2026-10-23Jayashanka · OPPEIN IT
INS-01

Installation rules

RuleRequirement
Wiring standardT568B at both ends: every surface box keystone and the cabinet patch panel
Run lengthPermanent link ≤ 90 m (longest here is 25 m)
Bend radiusNot tighter than 4 × the cable diameter (about 25 mm)
Pulling tensionNo more than 110 N (about 11 kg); pull by hand, do not use force
UntwistingKeep pair twists to within 13 mm of each termination
FixingVelcro ties only; no cable ties pulled tight and no staples
Power separationAt least 300 mm from unshielded power cables and light fittings; cross at 90°
CasingScrew-fix every 500 mm, level, at skirting height (desk height behind desks is fine); proper corner / tee fittings; fill no more than 60–70 %
Casing size25 × 16 mm: 13 m · 40 × 25 mm: 8 m (see DOC 01)
LidsFitted after the cables are tested; cut lids to meet the fittings with no gaps
Service loopsAbout 1 m inside the cabinet, 300 mm at each surface box
Ceiling conduitPVC conduit fixed above the ceiling (not resting on tiles), large bends only, draw wire left in; fill no more than 40 %
Through wallsSleeve the hole at mark 2 and at the cabinet, keep the bend radius, and re-seal after the cables are through
INS-02

Sequence of work

  1. Survey and mark out. Walk the site with this pack. Mark the casing line on the walls, the SW1 and SW2 desks, every surface box and the two AP positions. Close items A-01 to A-06.
  2. Conduit and casing. Mount the cabinet. Check (or fit) the ceiling PVC conduit from the cabinet to mark 2 and from mark 2 to AP2, with a draw wire. Fix the vertical casing behind the wall at mark 2, the wall casing to J4, the branches to each desk, AP1's casing up the wall, and the IT Room casing. Leave the lids off.
  3. Pull cables. Pull all 14 cables from the cabinet: the Design-area bundle through the ceiling conduit to mark 2 in one pull (use the draw wire), then lay each one into the casing to its desk; C-14 is the phone line, so label it TEL. Label both ends before cutting from the box.
  4. Terminate. Punch down the 14 cabinet cables on the patch panel and every other end on a keystone in its surface box. Fit labels.
  5. Test, then close. Test every link and fill in the test log (DOC 09 · Testing & handover). Fix and retest failures, then fit the casing lids.
  6. Active equipment. Fit the shelves, ONT, router, SW0, SW1, SW2, the PoE injectors and the PDU. Patch router L1 → SW0 P1, SW0 P2 → SW1, SW0 P3 → SW2, then every panel port to its switch port as in DOC 02 (AP cables through their injectors) and the phone line to ONT TEL1 with an RJ11–RJ45 cord. Mount and connect the access points.
  7. Configure and commission. Set up router, switch and Wi-Fi to the agreed plan. Check every device online.
  8. Handover. Update this pack to "as built", hand over the test results, passwords (separately) and keys.
INS-03

Site coordination

  • Casing goes on the walls, so desks and cabinets along the route need to be pulled out on install day.
  • The electrician provides the cabinet power socket (the 8-way PDU feeds the ONT, router, 3 switches and 2 PoE injectors) and the desk power sockets before termination day.
  • The ISP installs the fibre and ONT into the IT Room; agree the entry point so the ONT reaches the U11 shelf.
  • A ladder is needed at AP1 and AP2 only.
