DOC 08 · INSTALLATION METHOD

Installation method

Rules for the installer and the order of work. The aim is one visit with the ceiling open, every cable pulled, and nothing left to guess about later.

Pyramid Centre · Office 14Rev A · 2026-10-16Jayashanka · OPPEIN IT
INS-01

Installation rules

RuleRequirement
Wiring standardT568B at both ends, on every outlet and the patch panel
Run lengthPermanent link ≤ 90 m (longest here is 24 m)
Bend radiusNot tighter than 4 × the cable diameter (about 25 mm)
Pulling tensionNo more than 110 N (about 11 kg); pull by hand, do not use force
UntwistingKeep pair twists to within 13 mm of each termination
FixingVelcro ties only; no cable ties pulled tight and no staples
Power separationAt least 300 mm from unshielded power cables and light fittings; cross at 90°
CeilingCables lie in the tray or on J-hooks every 1.2–1.5 m; never on the ceiling tiles or grid
Service loopsAbout 1 m inside the cabinet, 300 mm behind each outlet
Fire stoppingRe-seal any wall or ceiling penetrations after the cables are through
INS-02

Sequence of work

  1. Survey and mark out. Walk the site with this pack. Mark every outlet and AP position; confirm the IT Room cabinet wall and the tray route. Close items A-01 to A-05.
  2. Containment. Mount the cabinet. Fit the ceiling tray / J-hooks from above the IT Room door to J5. Fit back boxes or surface boxes at each outlet.
  3. Pull cables. Pull all 12 cables in one go. Label both ends before cutting from the box.
  4. Terminate. Punch down on the patch panel in port order and on the keystones at each outlet. Fit faceplates and labels.
  5. Test. Test every link and fill in the test log (DOC 09 · Testing & handover). Fix and retest any failures.
  6. Active equipment. Fit the shelf, ONT, router, switch and PDU. Patch with 0.5 m cords, port n to port n. Mount and connect the access points.
  7. Configure and commission. Set up router, switch and Wi-Fi to the agreed plan. Check every device online.
  8. Handover. Update this pack to "as built", hand over the test results, passwords (separately) and keys.
INS-03

Site coordination

  • Book the cabling while the ceiling is open; the pull is the only step that needs the ceiling.
  • The electrician provides the cabinet power socket and the desk power sockets before termination day.
  • The ISP installs the fibre and ONT into the IT Room; agree the entry point so the ONT reaches the U11 shelf.
  • Keep a clear path between the IT Room door and the Design area on pull day.
