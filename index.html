About Me

I'm Jayashanka Anushan, a Robotics Engineer and MSc researcher based in Dubai, UAE. I build at the intersection of software, hardware, and infrastructure — from ROS-based control systems and embedded prototypes to cloud-connected robots and smart operational automation.

My research focuses on humanoid robotics and Human-Robot Interaction (HRI) — specifically building emotionally intelligent systems that perceive human behaviour through multimodal sensing (audio, vision, environmental signals) and respond naturally.

Beyond research, I deploy real-world automation: smart warehouses, cloud-connected robots via AWS + ROSBridge, logistics automation, and smart infrastructure installations. If it blends robots + ops + impact, I'm in. 🤖⚡

"Robots will not replace humans — they will extend human capability."
